The 14-Step LLD Interview Roadmap

Never get stuck or jump straight into messy code. Follow this structured 45-minute interview roadmap to systematically architect any complex system in C++.

PHASE 1: SCOPING (MINUTES 0 - 10)

1. Clarify Requirements & Actors

  • Step 1: Clarify scope (Core vs Out-of-scope).
  • Step 2: Identify actors (Admin, Customer, System).
  • Step 3: List non-functional constraints (Latency, Concurrency, Memory).
PHASE 2: DOMAIN MODELING (MINUTES 10 - 20)

2. Entities, Responsibilities & Relationships

  • Step 4: Identify tangible domain entities & value objects.
  • Step 5: Assign single responsibilities (CRC method).
  • Step 6: Define relationships (Composition vs Aggregation vs Association).
  • Step 7: Draw live UML Class Diagram.
PHASE 3: DESIGN PATTERNS & INTERFACES (MINUTES 20 - 30)

3. SOLID & Pattern Integration

  • Step 8: Design abstract base interfaces (pure virtual = 0).
  • Step 9: Apply SOLID principles (DIP, OCP, LSP).
  • Step 10: Select patterns (Strategy for algorithms, Factory for creation, Observer for events).
PHASE 4: C++ CODING & TRADEOFFS (MINUTES 30 - 45)

4. Idiomatic C++ & Deep Defense

  • Step 11: Implement core classes with smart pointers (`unique_ptr`, `shared_ptr`).
  • Step 12: Handle edge cases and invalid states (exceptions vs optional).
  • Step 13: Address concurrency (`std::mutex`, `std::lock_guard`, race prevention).
  • Step 14: Discuss trade-offs and future extensibility.
